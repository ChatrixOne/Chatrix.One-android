Chatrix.One is a friendly, private messenger for chatrix.one and any other Jabber/XMPP server. It is based on Conversations by Daniel Gultsch and keeps all of its features, adding its own look and faster ways to work with messages.

<b>What Chatrix.One adds</b>
* Swipe left to quote, swipe right to react with your favourite emoji, double-tap to copy. Every gesture is configurable, including editing your own message.
* Compact long-press menu with a reaction row, the full emoji picker and emoji typing.
* Chatrix.One design with rounded Material 3 dialogs, chat backgrounds per chat and voice messages shown as waveforms.
* Clear connection status icon and a one-tap way to hide the permanent notification.
* Crash reports only after asking, via XMPP or email.

<b>Everything from Conversations</b>
* End-to-end encryption with OMEMO or OpenPGP
* Encrypted audio and video calls
* Send and receive images, files, voice messages and locations
* Private group chats and public channels
* Emoji reactions, read markers, message correction
* Multiple accounts, works with every modern XMPP server

Chatrix.One contains no trackers, no advertising and uses no Google services. It installs next to Conversations, and you can move your chats with a Conversations backup.
